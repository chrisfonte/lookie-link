A numbered key keeps a dense drawing readable

When several labels crowd one small drawing, put numbers on the drawing and the words beside it. The key is ordinary text: it wraps, it can carry a full sentence, and the drawing fits a phone with no sideways scrolling. This control panel is fictional.

Keep content local; share the framing

Use HTML/CSS for steps and comparison cards, inline SVG for precise connections or spatial annotations, and an illustration for appearance or texture. Keep essential facts in text as well. Draw only what the source confirms: a drawing asserts where things are, so check placement against the source before trusting a label.

The reusable classes supply surfaces, readable labels, leaders, scrolling and captions. Coordinates and subject-specific colors belong to the drawing. If a color is itself the subject, preserve it and label it; status meaning uses semantic tokens plus text.
