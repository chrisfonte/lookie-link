Keep content local; share the framing

Use HTML/CSS for steps and comparison cards, inline SVG for precise connections or spatial annotations, and an illustration for appearance or texture. Keep essential facts in text as well.

The reusable classes supply surfaces, readable labels, leaders, scrolling and captions. Coordinates and subject-specific colors belong to the drawing. If a color is itself the subject, preserve it and label it; status meaning uses semantic tokens plus text.
